I'm Yile Liu, an undergraduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the ZJU-UIUC Institute, currently in Haining, China. From Fall 2027 I'll be on the Urbana-Champaign campus.

My interests are compilers and low-level systems — I like problems where the output has to actually run, and where "it works" is something you can measure.

Work

LLVMIRToLC3Pass — an out-of-tree LLVM pass, written in C++, that lowers LLVM IR generated from C down to LC-3 assembly, a 16-bit ISA with eight registers. It covers phi, select, switch, getelementptr, variadic calls, and a small libc (printf, scanf, sprintf, sscanf). It is tested by 35 cases in three tiers; 16 of them assemble the generated code and run it on an LC-3 simulator, comparing stdout against expected output.

Before that

Competitive programming (Chinese OI), 2019–2023. The habit I kept from it is measuring constant factors instead of trusting asymptotics.
